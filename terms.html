1. Thermal Control Software

MacTC modifies or evaluates fan behavior and related thermal-management behavior on supported Mac hardware. In System mode, MacTC evaluates fan behavior while macOS controls the fans. When MacTC mode is enabled and active fan control is available, its purpose is to pursue lower operating temperatures, typically at the cost of more fan activity, more fan noise, and potentially more power use.
